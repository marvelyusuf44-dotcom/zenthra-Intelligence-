What Zenthra does

One research loop, every question.

Zenthra isn't a dashboard you search — it's an agent that investigates. Ask a question, and it works through market data, on-chain signals, and derivatives context, cross-checks what it finds, and gives you an answer with the evidence attached.

No trade gets executed and no number gets invented — deterministic calculations stay outside the language model, and anything Zenthra can't verify, it says so plainly instead of guessing.

Evidence-first intelligence

If Zenthra can't verify it, it says so.

Every research answer shows what backed it — and what didn't. When a data source isn't connected, Zenthra shows "unavailable," never a plausible-sounding guess dressed up as fact.

That's a deliberate trade: it means some categories start at zero until real sources are wired up — and it means when Zenthra does give you a number, it's real.
